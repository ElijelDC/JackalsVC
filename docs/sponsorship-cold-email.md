## Tips

- Add one personal line about why them. For Meakstown / Charlestown / Finglas: *“We run a lot of weekly volleyball sessions in Meakstown…”*
- Don’t list package prices in cold emails — send them to the site first.
- Packages (for follow-ups if asked): Club Partner €150 · Spotlight Partner €500 · Matchday & Kit Partner €1,000.

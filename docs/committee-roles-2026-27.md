# Jackals VC — Committee Roles 2026/27

Use the **short list** for WhatsApp / online sign-up. Use the **detailed list** when handing a role to someone.

---

## Short list (online / handout)

1. **Equipment Officer** — balls, nets, gear checks; help set up hall on home days  
2. **Volunteer Coordinator** — scorers, linespeople, helpers for home games + big club days  
3. **Player Welfare Officer** — first contact for players with club issues  
4. **Children’s Officer** — first contact for U18 concerns  
5. **Social Media Officer** — post on Instagram / club pages  
6. **GFX Designer** — Canva Pro graphics; keep Jackals look consistent (can build a small design team)  
7. **Club Announcements Lead** — WhatsApp updates (merch, sponsors, events)  
8. **Events Officer** — plan socials / club nights; pass details to Announcements  
9. **Photo Officer** — matchday / training photos for Socials + GFX Designer  
10. **Membership Officer** — remind members to pay; use the website to see who’s due / unpaid  
11. **Secretary** — committee meetings, minutes, correspondence, and club records  
12. **Ordinary Committee Member** — general committee support; help with decisions and club jobs as needed  

---

## Detailed responsibilities

### 1. Equipment Officer
- Keep a simple count of club balls (match + training).
- Check nets, antennae, posts, and scoreboard are usable.
- Flag damaged / worn kit early so it can be replaced before a home day.
- Help set up the hall on home matchdays (nets, lines, benches, score table).
- Tell the committee what needs buying or repairing.

### 2. Volunteer Coordinator
- Before each home matchday, fill scorers and linespeople (and any other helpers needed).
- Share a clear rota / WhatsApp ask so people know times and roles.
- Cover last-minute dropouts where possible.
- Work with Equipment Officer and Events Officer so setup and staffing aren’t left to one person.

### 3. Player Welfare Officer
- Be a clear, approachable point of contact for players with club issues (not coaching tactics).
- Listen and escalate to the committee when needed (fees stress, conflict, inclusion, access).
- Keep conversations respectful and confidential where appropriate.
- Signpost U18 concerns to the Children’s Officer (do not handle child safeguarding alone).
- Help new or quieter players feel they have someone to talk to.

### 4. Children’s Officer
- Be the named contact for any U18 / parent concerns.
- Make sure U18 processes the club needs are followed (forms, consent, Garda vetting where required).
- Keep a simple list of U18 players and parent/guardian contacts if the club uses one.
- Escalate serious concerns through the proper safeguarding route (do not ignore or “fix informally”).
- Work with coaches and Player Welfare Officer so under-18s are looked after properly.

### 5. Social Media Officer
- Post regularly on club social pages (results, fixtures reminders, events, recruitment).
- Use graphics from GFX Designer so posts look consistent.
- Use photos from Photo Officer when available.
- Keep tone friendly and on-brand; no personal drama in club channels.
- Coordinate big announcements with Club Announcements Lead so WhatsApp and social match.

### 6. GFX Designer
- Create social and promo graphics in Canva Pro.
- Keep colours, fonts, logo use, and layout consistent across Jackals posts.
- Build / maintain simple reusable templates (matchday, result, event, merch, sponsor).
- Supply finished assets to Social Media Officer on time.
- Support Events / Sponsors / Merch graphics when asked.
- Optional: set up a small design team within the club (e.g. 2–3 people helping with posts, matchday graphics, event artwork) and coordinate them so everything stays on-brand.

### 7. Club Announcements Lead
- Post clear WhatsApp updates for players (new merch, sponsors, events, important club news).
- Collect info from other officers (Events, Membership, Socials, committee) before posting.
- Keep messages short, timed, and accurate — one source of truth in the main chat.
- Avoid spam; batch updates when it makes sense.
- Point people to the right officer if they reply with questions.

### 8. Events Officer
- Plan club socials and club nights (venue, date, rough budget, who’s invited).
- Coordinate helpers with Volunteer Coordinator if staffing is needed.
- Pass confirmed event details to Club Announcements Lead and Social Media Officer.
- Ask GFX Designer for event graphics when needed.
- After the event, note what worked for next time.

### 9. Photo Officer
- Take photos at matchdays and (when useful) training / events.
- Share usable photos promptly with Social Media Officer and GFX Designer.
- Aim for a mix: action, team, setup, people — not only one style.
- Respect privacy (especially U18s / anyone who asks not to be posted).
- Keep a simple shared folder so posts aren’t blocked waiting for images.

### 10. Membership Officer
- Remind members to pay membership on time.
- Use the club website account to see who is due / unpaid.
- Send polite chase messages (WhatsApp / agreed channel) without public shaming.
- Flag long-overdue cases to the Treasurer / committee.
- Keep the committee updated on payment progress (e.g. how many still outstanding).

### 11. Secretary
- Organise committee meetings (date, agenda, and invites).
- Take clear minutes and share them with the committee after meetings.
- Keep simple club records in order (contacts, role holders, key docs).
- Handle or forward official club correspondence when needed.
- Support AGM / EGM prep (notices, agenda, attendance notes).

### 12. Ordinary Committee Member
- Attend committee meetings and contribute to decisions.
- Help with club jobs when asked (events, matchdays, admin tasks).
- Support other officers when their workload spikes.
- Bring ideas and feedback from players to the committee.
- Step in on small tasks so the club doesn’t rely on only a few people.

Season rhythm

  • Bye week: Full gym Mon/Fri, Mon 4×3 standing + block jumps, Fri power/hang clean 4×3.
  • Match week: Mon reduced 2×3 jumps; Fri = light legs (Sun match) OR home activation (Sat match).
  • Deload: 30 Sep–4 Oct pre-MD1 + every 4th training week → 2×3 jumps, 2–3 main lift sets.
  • Sat 10 Oct 2026: No Flyefit · prehab + easy wall sets · Sun 11 Oct MD1 13:00 vs BMP Titans (Home, warm-up 12:30).
  • Rules: No heavy legs within 48 h of NL kick-off · Wed club = hard day · No catch-up gym · Emergency: vert down 2 weeks OR knee >3/10 OR 3 bad sleep nights → 5 days prehab + Wed only.

15-minute RAMP warmup

After optional daily prehab at gym/home.

  • Flyefit Monday (bye): Raise 3 min bike/row · Activate 4 min TKE, bridge, hip abduction, balance, band ER · Mobilize 3 min swings/ankles/90-90/open books · Potentiate 5 min empty RDL, hang high pull ramp, leg press warm sets, 2 easy reaches.
  • Flyefit Monday (in-season/deload): Raise 3 min · Activate TKE/bridge/ER/calf · Mobilize swings/ankles/scap · Potentiate clean ramp, 1 press set, 1 easy + 1×80% jump.
  • Thursday pull: 3 min row/bike · 4 min band/scap/dead bug/wrist · 4 min T-spine · 4 min light pulldown + row ramp.
  • Friday bye: 3 min bike · 4 min TKE/glute/ham/balance · 3 min hinge · 5 min RDL + clean + light press ramp.
  • Friday light (Sun match): 3 min bike · 5 min activate · 4 min mobilize · 3 min RDL empty + 50%×5 only.
  • Home Tue/Sat/eve: 2–3 min march · 5 min wrists/ER/knees · 3–4 min mobility · 3–4 min easy wall sets.
  • Wed club: 3 min jog · 4 min knee/hip/ER · 4 min mobility · team warm-up (submax jumps only if knee ≤3/10).
  • Match / Sunday track: Prehab 12 min + team warm-up OR prehab + log vert.
